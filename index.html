Own your .names.

Namehold is a desktop wallet for Handshake: hold HNS, win and renew top-level names, edit their DNS, and buy or sell them on the Shakedex market. Your recovery phrase stays on your computer.

Download Read the source macOS, Windows, Linux · open source, MIT

Every step of a name, in one place

  1. openStart the auction
  2. bidAbout 5 days, sealed
  3. revealAbout 10 days. The app reminds you
  4. registerWin it, set its DNS
  5. renewHundreds at once, if you hold them
  6. transferSend it, or sell it

All your names, and what each one needs next

The Names page shows every name you own, bid on or watch, with its next step and how long you have.

  • Bid, reveal, renew, finalize or transfer in batches
  • Edit DNS records with a typed editor
  • A note on names written with look-alike letters

Buy and sell names without a middleman

Namehold trades through Shakedex. Before you buy, your own node checks each listing against the chain.

  • Buy Now and reverse auctions
  • List your names and lower a price later
  • See each purchase through to finalize

One page for each name

See where a name stands, what happened to it and what you can do with it now, including a flag for names also applied for in ICANN's 2026 round.

Your keys stay with you

  • Encrypted on your computer. A new mainnet wallet needs a passphrase of at least 8 characters.
  • Secrets stay in a secure window. Your phrase and passphrase are typed outside the main app window.
  • Ledger support. With a Ledger Nano, the device signs and you confirm on its screen.

You choose whom to trust

  • Your own node. Run hsd from the app, or point it at one you trust. Sending needs a node.
  • Read without one. Check balances and names through an explorer while you get set up.
  • Open source. Read the code and check every release file against its signature.

Beta software. Transactions, transfers and name sales are irreversible. Test with one name or a small amount first. The macOS and Windows builds are not code-signed, so check your download:

shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing

The README shows how to verify the signature and the build's origin too.
