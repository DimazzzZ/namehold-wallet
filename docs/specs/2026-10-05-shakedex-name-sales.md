# Shakedex name sales

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-05), amended 2026-10-08 for the selling stage (R31, R32, manual lock recovery, the daemon's publishing contract); buying implemented; selling implemented (the stack #83–#92); the clean-up implemented; amended 2026-10-21 for finding locks after a restore (R34), implemented. Supersedes [2026-09-21 Paid name swaps](./2026-09-21-paid-name-swaps.md). Decisions: [ADR 0003](../adr/0003-own-shakedex-implementation.md) (own implementation), [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-shakedex-lock-keys-from-seed.md) (lock keys from the seed). Delivered in three stages — buying, selling, and the clean-up that removes the withdrawn paid-swap code — see Pointers.

## 1. Summary

Selling a Handshake name for HNS should take one transaction that either pays the seller and commits the name to the buyer, or does neither. Shakedex does that, and the one live name market, LearnHNS Market, runs on it; Bob Wallet and the shakedex CLI speak the same listing file. Namehold can do neither today, so its users are locked out of the only live market. This spec makes Namehold a full Shakedex participant: browse and buy listings from LearnHNS Market or a listing file, and sell names as Buy Now listings or reverse auctions that any Shakedex-compatible wallet can buy. Everything a buyer relies on is checked locally, against the profile's node; the market is a convenience, never a source of trust.

## 2. Terms

Listing, Pending listing, Buy Now, Reverse auction, Price step, Current step, Listing file, Lock, Lock key, Purchase, Unconfirmed purchase, Awaiting finalize, Sold, Market fee, Cancel, Lower price, Locking, Restored lock and My listings are defined in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md) under "Name sales (Shakedex)". Shakedex's own words — auction, bid, presign, proof, fill — appear only at the protocol boundary (`noncustodial/shakedex/`, the `proof.json` URL), because in this wallet a bid is a buyer's sealed offer in a name auction, the opposite direction of a Shakedex "bid".

The protocol facts behind this spec are pinned to hsd v8.0.0, shakedex `2c4fa04eab68a528e758598d11b5da5666113b11` and learnhns-market `3d117361166c806e587752f56b44b9ab13abefc3`. Anything a test depends on is copied into the repository (R1, R2).

### 3.1 Protocol

**R1 — Byte-identical with hsd.** The lock script `OP_TYPE TRANSFER EQUAL IF <pub33> CHECKSIG ELSE OP_TYPE FINALIZE EQUAL ENDIF`, its P2WSH address (program = SHA3-256 of the script), the price-step template, its `0x84` (`ANYONECANPAY|SINGLEREVERSE`) signature, the purchase, the unsigned FINALIZE out of the lock (witness `[lockScript]`) and the `0x83` (`ANYONECANPAY|SINGLE`) cancel are reproduced byte for byte against vectors generated by hsd v8.0.0.
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/shakedex/{script,template,purchase}.rs`, on `noncustodial/tx.rs`; selling adds `noncustodial/shakedex/{sell,cancel}.rs` and a P2WSH signing path next to `sign_p2wpkh_input` (`Transaction::sign_p2wsh_input`).
*Pinned:* `tests/shakedex_vector_tests.rs`, vectors generated into `src-tauri/tests/vectors/` by an extension of `gen_hsd_vectors.js` (the research vector covers signing price steps and the purchase only; the buyer's FINALIZE is added there, and selling adds the seller's steps, the FINALIZE into the lock, the cancel and its FINALIZE: `shakedex_vector_tests::{seller_steps_match_hsd, lock_finalize_matches_hsd_signed_hex, cancel_matches_hsd_signed_hex, cancel_finalize_matches_hsd_signed_hex}`, `noncustodial::tx` unit test `sign_p2wsh_input_matches_hsd`, `noncustodial::shakedex::sell` unit test `sign_step_is_0x84_low_s_and_verifies`).

**R2 — Listing file is Shakedex v2 JSON, read strictly.** Only `version: 2`. Every price step carries a 65-byte low-S signature whose last byte is exactly `0x84` — shakedex accepts any sighash type that verifies, this wallet does not. `feeAddr` with `fee == 0` means no fee output. `expiresAt` is read and shown, never enforced: a listing file writes it in seconds, the Market feed as a naive UTC ISO-8601 string, and both are read; anything else is refused. The name must be a valid Handshake name (`names::verify_name`). Unknown fields are preserved on round-trip and not interpreted. Input over 256 KiB is refused before parsing. A step's lock time must fit R3's 40-bit encoding, and its price plus fee must not exceed the money supply (`MAX_MONEY`). `paymentAddr` must be 20-byte version-0 under this profile's prefix, so a mainnet file cannot be bought from a regtest profile; an unusable `feeAddr` does not refuse the file, it only pays no fee (R11).
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/shakedex/listing_file.rs`.
*Pinned:* `tests/shakedex_listing_file_tests.rs` (including `wrong_sighash_byte_refused`, `high_s_signature_refused_at_import`, `other_network_listing_names_its_network`, `invalid_name_refused_at_import`, `unencodable_lock_time_or_amount_above_money_supply_refused_at_import`, `live_market_feed_rows_parse_with_their_iso_expiry`, `unreadable_expiry_refused`), live LearnHNS fixtures (`dexreviews`, `cooljobs`) committed under `src-tauri/tests/vectors/shakedex/`, round-trip unchanged.

**R3 — Validity of a price step follows consensus exactly.** A step's lock time is encoded as `0x80000000 | floor(lockTime / 512)` and the step is valid in the next block when `floor(lockTime / 512) * 512 < MTP`, where MTP is the tip's `mediantime` (`getblockchaininfo`) — strictly less, and up to 511 s before the nominal time, which is why every step this wallet signs after the first has a lock time rounded up to a multiple of 512 (R19, R24). The template signs input 0 with sequence `0xfffffffe`; with `0xffffffff` hsd treats the transaction as final regardless of lock time and every future step would be buyable at once. The **current step** is the cheapest step valid by this rule across the original schedule and every Lower price step.
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/shakedex/template.rs::{encode_lock_time, is_valid_at}`, `noncustodial/rpc.rs` (`BlockchainInfo` gains `mediantime`).
*Pinned:* `noncustodial::shakedex::template` unit tests `{boundary_at_mtp, sequence_is_fffffffe}`.

**R4 — Fees are charged on vsize, including foreign witnesses.** The purchase (seller's input witness `[sig65, lockScript]`), the buyer's FINALIZE (`[lockScript]`), the cancel and the cancel's FINALIZE are sized with their real witnesses, not the P2WPKH constants, at the profile's fee rate with a floor of 5000 doos/kB as shakedex uses.
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/shakedex/funding.rs` (the fee is charged on the vsize of the plan signed in full, foreign witness included, with the floor `funding::SHAKEDEX_MIN_RATE_PER_BYTE`), used by `purchase.rs`, `sell.rs` and `cancel.rs`.
*Pinned:* `noncustodial::shakedex::purchase` unit test `fee_covers_hsd_vsize_with_foreign_witness`, and `shakedex_vector_tests::{purchase_plan_matches_hsd_signed_hex, purchase_finalize_plan_matches_hsd_signed_hex}` against hsd-signed transactions; selling adds `noncustodial::shakedex::cancel` unit test `fee_covers_hsd_vsize_with_the_lock_witness`, and on a live node `tests/live_node_it.rs::{shakedex_lock_finalize_pays_its_fee_rate_on_vsize, shakedex_cancel_pays_its_fee_rate_on_vsize}`.

### 3.2 Buying

**R5 — Two ways in, one way through.** A listing comes from the Market tab (LearnHNS Market, `GET /api/v2/auctions`), a listing file, pasted JSON, or a `market.learnhns.com/listing/<name>` link (fetched as `/listing/<name>/proof.json`). Arbitrary URLs are not fetched, and a link whose file names another name is refused. Every path produces a listing file and goes through R7 and R10.
*Enforced:* `market/learnhns.rs`, `commands/shakedex.rs::shakedex_import_listing`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_cmd_tests::{import_text_and_file, import_link_fetches_listing_file, import_link_only_learnhns, import_link_refuses_a_file_for_another_name}`, `learnhns_tests::only_learnhns_listing_links` (each source; a non-LearnHNS URL is refused).

**R6 — Buying needs a node that can send.** `LocalNode`, or `RemoteNode` with "Allow sending via remote node" — the existing write capability (`WriteCapability::evaluate`), now also checked when the purchase draft is built, not only at broadcast. Profiles other than recovery-phrase ones (Ledger, watch-only, extended private key) cannot buy (R16).
*Enforced:* `commands/shakedex.rs::software_writer_ctx`, used by `shakedex_build_purchase_draft` and `shakedex_build_purchase_finalize_draft`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_cmd_tests::build_purchase_refused_without_write_capability`, `shakedex_finalize_tests::finalize_refused_without_write_capability`.

**R7 — Verified locally, on the profile's node.** Before Buy is offered:
- the listing file passes R2;
- the lock script is `lock_script(publicKey)` and SHA3-256 of it equals the lock coin's program;
- the lock coin, fetched with `GET /coin/:hash/:index` (a new REST wrapper — `gettxout` carries no covenant and returns null for a spent coin, the same as for one it does not know), exists, is unspent, carries FINALIZE with `items[2] == name`;
- the lock outpoint is the name's current owner in `getnameinfo`, and the coin's covenant height equals the name's height — otherwise the name expired and was re-registered and the lock coin is a leftover;
- each price step's signature is valid ECDSA over `signature_hash(0, lockScript, coin.value, 0x84)` of the template, with `price > 0`.

A node reply that leaves out a field these checks read (the coin's address or covenant, the covenant's name and height items, the name's `info`, owner, height, renewal or claimed) means the listing could not be checked, never that it failed. What the market says about availability is ignored. A spent or leftover lock coin is not a safety issue — the network would reject the purchase and nothing would be paid — but such listings are not offered.
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/shakedex/verify.rs`, `noncustodial/rpc.rs::get_coin`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_verify_tests` (tampered price, wrong lock address, spent coin, re-registered name, covenant height mismatch, and every missing field as "could not check": `lock_coin_without_address_or_covenant_could_not_be_checked`, `name_without_owner_could_not_be_checked`, `name_without_height_could_not_be_checked`, `name_reply_without_info_key_could_not_be_checked`, `lock_coin_covenant_without_readable_name_or_height_could_not_be_checked`), and a 404 that is not hsd's empty one is not a spent coin (`rpc::tests::get_coin_404_with_a_body_is_an_error_not_spent`); the sighash byte and the network prefix are refused by R2's parser.

**R8 — What can be bought, and what is hidden.** The Market list shows only listings that pass R7 and R9, with a price and Buy. A listing the node could not answer for (`Hidden::CouldNotCheck`) is listed too, without a price and without Buy, as "Couldn't check" with Retry: the backend sends its listing file with the hidden row (`HiddenRow.listingJson`), and Retry verifies that file again through `shakedex_import_listing`. Every other hidden listing is left out of the list, behind a toggle at its foot that counts them by reason ("▸ 5 unavailable listings hidden: 3 sold, 1 failed the check, 1 not valid yet", zero parts left out) and expands to each name with its reason, without Buy. The list loads the market a hundred listings at a time (`MarketPage.{page, pageCount}`, from the feed's `total`), Load more appending the next page to the same list; the toggle counts every page loaded. In SPV and Explorer modes, where R7 cannot run (`MarketPage.verified` false), the list shows no rows and says listings can't be checked yet.
*Enforced:* `commands/shakedex.rs::shakedex_list_market`, `src/components/market/marketModel.ts::{buildList, hiddenToggleText}`, `src/components/market/MarketPage.tsx`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_cmd_tests::{list_market_survives_one_lookup_failure, list_market_counts_unparsable_rows_as_failed}` (the listing file of a row that could not be checked, none for one that did not parse), `market-model.test.ts` (`buildList`, `hiddenToggleText`), `market-page.test.tsx :: counts each hidden reason in the footer and lists them when expanded, unselectable`, `market-page.test.tsx :: lists a listing the node could not check without a price, and Retry checks it again`, `market-page.test.tsx :: appends the next page to the same list, then says every listing is loaded`, `market-page.test.tsx :: says listings cannot be checked without a full or remote node (SPV, Explorer)`, and on a live node `tests/live_node_it.rs::shakedex_listing_not_valid_yet_becomes_buyable` (a listing signed here with a step ahead of the median time).

**R9 — A name that would expire before it can be finalized cannot be bought.** Only CLAIM, REGISTER, RENEW and FINALIZE restart a name's renewal clock; a purchase (TRANSFER) does not. Using hsd's claim-aware expiry end for the name, if it falls at or before `tip + 1 + transferLockup + 1 day` (in blocks, from the network's parameters; mainnet 288 + 144), the listing cannot be bought: the Market list hides it (R8), an import of it is refused with the reason and not listed, and building the purchase refuses it. "1 day" is 144 blocks, or the transfer lockup itself where that is shorter (regtest's 10 blocks, simnet's 5; values from `noncustodial/network.rs`). On regtest, whose renewal window is 5000 blocks, a 144-block day would make the warning window 4320 blocks and warn on most of a name's life; with the lockup as the day it is 300. If it falls within 30 such days after that, Buy stays enabled with a warning that forgetting to finalize loses the name.
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/network.rs::NameParams::expiry_end` (the one expiry rule, also behind Renewals and the name actions), `noncustodial/shakedex/verify.rs::finalize_deadline_ok`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_verify_tests::{expiring_name_blocked_and_warned_mainnet, expiring_name_regtest_margin_is_relative, name_about_to_expire_is_hidden, name_expiring_within_a_month_is_buyable_with_warning}`, `market-page.test.tsx :: says why an imported listing cannot be bought, and keeps it out of the list`, `listing-detail.test.tsx :: warns when the name expires soon after it can be finalized`, and on a live node `tests/live_node_it.rs::shakedex_listing_expiring_before_finalize_is_not_offered` (the boundary block on both sides, against hsd's own `renewalPeriodEnd`).

**R10 — The buyer pays exactly the current step, and sees every line.** Before Buy, the Market's listing panel shows the price to the seller, the market fee (if paid) in HNS and as a percentage of the price in exact hundredths, the network fee and the total, and says the name becomes the buyer's after a finalize, the network's transfer lockup in blocks after the purchase is mined, with its approximate time where miners set the pace (288 blocks, about two days, on mainnet; no time on regtest and simnet, which mine on demand). Both texts come from one place (`purchase::{fee_percent_text, finalize_wait_text}`), so the panel and the secure window cannot disagree. The secure spend confirmation gets the same rows; its summary is extended for purchases, which must not count the name's own value as money spent. The current step is re-checked against MTP just before broadcast; if it changed, or the node cannot say, the draft is discarded and the user is asked again. The paid step is told by its lock time (the one the purchase is signed with), not by its price: a listing may hold several steps at one price (the shakedex CLI writes one when a reverse auction's start and end prices are equal), and another of them being valid does not make the purchase final.
*Enforced:* `commands/tx.rs::confirm_details_for_draft`, `src/components/market/ListingDetail.tsx`, `src/components/market/usePurchase.ts::usePurchase`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_cmd_tests::{confirm_rows_for_purchase, confirm_rows_show_the_stored_finalize_wait, broadcast_refused_when_a_cheaper_step_became_valid, broadcast_refused_when_the_paid_step_is_no_longer_valid, broadcast_refused_when_only_another_step_at_the_same_price_is_valid, broadcast_refused_when_the_recorded_price_is_unreadable}`, `noncustodial::shakedex::purchase` unit tests `{fee_percent_text_is_exact_to_the_hundredth, finalize_wait_text_times_only_networks_with_miners}`, `listing-detail.test.tsx` (`ListingDetail: the breakdown`, `ListingDetail: buying`), and on a live node `tests/live_node_it.rs::shakedex_purchase_at_a_same_price_step_not_valid_is_not_sent` (the median time taken back between two steps at one price).

**R11 — The market fee is paid only when it is the market's own.** `fee` and `feeAddr` are not covered by the seller's signature, and anyone can upload a copy of a listing to LearnHNS with any fee. The "Pay the market fee" checkbox is on by default only when the listing came from LearnHNS (the renderer says so; it already decides the checkbox, and a fee it calls the market's must still match the market's own address and rate), `feeAddr` equals the address in its `GET /api/v2/fee_info`, and `fee <= floor(rate × price / 10000)` for the rate published there. Otherwise — file or paste, a different address, or a higher fee — it is off, with "not signed by the seller and not the market's published fee — anyone could have added it". A missing or invalid `feeAddr` adds no fee output, and the line says why (upstream Bob 2.1.0 burns such a fee to the all-zero address). A fee below the dust limit adds no output either, for the same reason a relay would refuse it.
*Enforced:* `commands/shakedex.rs::decide_market_fee`, `market/learnhns.rs::{fee_info, market_fee_is_published}`.
*Pinned:* `shakedex_cmd_tests::{published_market_fee_is_paid_when_chosen, foreign_market_fee_is_not_paid_by_default, fee_from_a_file_is_never_published, unusable_fee_address_is_never_paid}`, `learnhns_tests::market_fee_must_match_published` (above the published rate), and on a live node `tests/live_node_it.rs::shakedex_accepted_market_fee_is_paid_exactly_on_chain` (the fee and the price as hsd reports the mined outputs).

**R12 — Reverse auctions are bought at the current step.** The listing shows the current price, the next step and when it becomes valid by R3, the floor, and the full schedule. There is no automatic buying when the price drops.
*Enforced:* `noncustodial/shakedex/template.rs::{current_step_index, next_step_index}` (the next step: of the steps not valid yet and cheaper than the current one, the soonest valid, the cheapest of those valid from the same time; the buyer's verdict, `verify.rs::verify_listing`, reads it), and for our own listing `noncustodial/shakedex/sell.rs::step_view` (the current price, the next one and the seconds of median time until it is valid, and the floor, from those two functions), which My listings reads at the node's median time (`commands/shakedex.rs::ListingPrices`).
*Pinned:* `noncustodial::shakedex::template` unit tests `{current_step_is_cheapest_valid, next_step_is_the_soonest_cheaper_step, next_step_of_steps_valid_together_is_the_cheapest}`, `shakedex_verify_tests::{next_step_is_the_earliest_cheaper_step_not_valid_yet, next_step_of_steps_valid_together_is_the_cheaper_one}`, `noncustodial::shakedex::sell` unit tests `{step_view_reads_now_next_and_floor_at_the_mtp, step_view_with_no_step_valid_yet_names_the_first_to_come, step_view_of_no_steps_is_none, step_view_of_a_lock_time_past_40_bits_is_a_corrupted_row}`, `shakedex_my_listings_tests::listing_prices_say_why_they_are_missing`, `listing-detail.test.tsx :: shows a reverse auction's steps from now on and how it drops` (the steps from the current one on, next and floor).

**R13 — A purchase is tracked from the moment it is sent, from the chain.** Before broadcast a `shakedex_purchases` row is written (listing file, txid, our name destination) in the same database transaction as the purchase draft, which reserves our funding outpoints. The name destination is a fresh receive address, and the row marks it used, so the next allocation skips it. A purchase draft never sent is discarded with its row once its coin reservation expires (`RESERVATION_TTL_SECS`, an hour): a broadcast refuses it from then on, and the sync deletes it only after a further send grace (`shakedex_jobs::SEND_GRACE_SECS`), so a send that started before the TTL never loses its row while it talks to the node. State is derived on every sync from what spends the lock coin and from the TRANSFER covenant, not from draft status:
- **Unconfirmed purchase:** our purchase is in the mempool.
- **Awaiting finalize · N blocks:** our purchase is mined and the TRANSFER at the lock address commits (covenant items 2–3) to our name destination; N = `purchase height + transferLockup − (tip + 1)`, because hsd judges the FINALIZE at the next block (`NameParams::blocks_until_finalize`).
- **Lost, nothing paid:** the lock coin is spent by anything else, hsd answered the broadcast with its own JSON-RPC error (in hsd 8.0.0 only for input it cannot parse; a reply that is not hsd's leaves the draft `broadcast_pending`), or the purchase was absent from the node's mempool and chain for 6 blocks, rebroadcast once, and still absent. Where it may not be rebroadcast (the daemon, or a remote node without the opt-in), it waits for a sync that may, but only until hsd's mempool expiry (72 hours, `MEMPOOL_EXPIRY_BLOCKS`) has passed: then it is lost too, its coins released. The rebroadcast re-checks the price like any broadcast (R10): if a cheaper step has become valid, the old price is not sent again and the purchase is lost here; if the paid step is not valid at the median time (it went back in a reorg), hsd would take the purchase as non-final and still answer with its txid, so nothing is sent and it waits like one that may not be rebroadcast; without the node's median time nothing is sent and nothing decided. Activity says nothing was paid. hsd 8.0.0's `sendrawtransaction` answers with the txid whatever its mempool does with the transaction (`rpc.js` relays it without awaiting the result; a purchase of a lock coin already spent is kept as an orphan), so a purchase someone else beat reads as sent and is lost once the job finds it missing with the lock coin spent.
- **Lost, paid:** our purchase is mined but its TRANSFER commits elsewhere, or the name expired (hsd's `"info": null`) or was won again before it was finalized. Activity shows the reason, and never says nothing was paid.

A mined purchase whose TRANSFER is spent on chain is never lost: only our FINALIZE can spend it, since the lock address's script allows a coin to be spent only into a TRANSFER signed by the seller or into a FINALIZE, and hsd never lets a TRANSFER be spent into another TRANSFER. With the name no longer at our destination, it is owned (the name moved on since) once that FINALIZE is found, mined, in our destination's history; no FINALIZE there, or one still in the mempool, gives no verdict. No purchase is declared lost while anything on chain still traces to it, and a node reply that leaves out a field the trace reads (the name's owner, `info`, the owner coin's address, the TRANSFER coin's covenant or its address items) leaves the purchase as it is until a later sync. On lost, our funding coins are released. A purchase lost while paying nothing is checked again for 7 days (hsd forgets a mempool transaction after 72 hours, but another node can still mine it, and a reorg can undo a competing purchase): once its TRANSFER is mined to us it is awaiting finalize again, and once its own transaction is mined and the name has been finalized to our destination since (anyone may send the FINALIZE) it is owned; either way its draft goes back to broadcasted. Until confirmed, the purchase's change is excluded from coin selection. A reorg moves the state back: an owned purchase is looked at again for 7 days, and if its TRANSFER coin is unspent again (the FINALIZE spends it), it is awaiting finalize again. Only our own funding inputs are reserved in `wallet_tx_drafts`; the seller's lock coin is not ours to reserve. The step runs from `run_sync_steps` in the app and in the daemon; only the app's sync rebroadcasts (SECURITY.md).
*Enforced:* migration `033_shakedex.sql` (`shakedex_purchases`), `shakedex_jobs.rs::refresh_purchases_step` run from `run_sync_steps`, `db/queries.rs::insert_tx_draft_reserving_coins_in_tx` with `DraftPlan::own_inputs` (own inputs only), `commands/tx.rs::classify_broadcast_outcome_with_client` (only hsd's own error fails a broadcast), `commands/tx.rs::refresh_tx_confirmations` (only hsd's own "not found" drops, fails or reverts a draft, and a purchase draft is never dropped or failed there: the purchase job gives it up after its rebroadcast).

**R14 — The purchased name is shown, and its finalize is offered.** Owned Names gains a source for purchases: the name appears as "Unconfirmed purchase", then "Awaiting finalize · N blocks", then owned once it arrives at our address. When N reaches 0: a notification and a Finalize button, in Owned Names and in the Market's Your purchases card, which lists every purchase not yet finalized with its price and its lockup's progress (`PurchaseNameState.{priceDoos, lockupBlocks}`, the network's transfer lockup from `noncustodial/network.rs`). **R22 — Sold means the lock coin was bought.** A listing is Sold when its lock coin is spent by a TRANSFER that commits to an address not ours, in a transaction whose payment output pays our listing address. A lock restored by name, which knows no payment address (R32), is Sold instead when its lock coin is spent, in a mined transaction, by a price step's signature (sighash `0x84`) into a TRANSFER of the name committing to an address not ours. Coins arriving at the payment address on their own mean nothing.
*Enforced:* `shakedex_jobs.rs::refresh_listings_step` from `run_sync_steps`, in the app and the daemon; its after-lock job reads each Finalizing, Listed, SalePending, Restored, Cancelling, CancelAwaitingFinalize and CancelFinalizing listing, and each Sold or Cancelled one within `AFTER_LOCK_RECHECK_DAYS`. hsd's 404 for the lock coin (spent in a block or the mempool) is never a verdict alone: the name is read, a mined TRANSFER out of the lock coin committing to an address of ours is a cancel and is judged first (R28), and a purchase is looked for (`shakedex_jobs.rs::find_sale`) only for a listing with a payment address and a lock outpoint. Every verdict rests on the purchase transaction found on chain and judged by `noncustodial/shakedex/sell.rs::purchase_in` against the listing's stored lock outpoint (every lock coin of a name sits at the same lock address, ADR 0004, so the address alone never says which listing was bought), and the lock coin written with the sale is the input `purchase_in` found. The transaction is found with `GET /tx` (mempool, or the index) or, on hsd's not-found, in the block at a known height (`getblockhash` + `getblock`, no index). Two ways lead to it: the name's owner first, when the wallet has a coin of that transaction at the payment address and hsd's `GET /coin` shows the owner as a TRANSFER of the name at our lock address committing to an address not ours (`sell::commitment_is_ours`; committing to ours it is a cancel, R28) and mined in a block, its transaction read at the owner coin's own height; otherwise each transaction that paid the listing's payment address (a coin of ours there in `tracked_utxos`, spent or not, which is a lead and never proof by itself), read at the height our coin of it was seen mined (a coin row whose transaction is in neither is no verdict). A transaction that cannot be read is logged and skipped, which can only withhold a verdict. Found in the mempool while the name's owner is still the lock coin: SalePending; mined while the owner has moved: Sold, with the purchase's txid (`sold_txid`), and a Sold listing whose purchase is still that txid is not written again, so it leaves the after-lock set once `AFTER_LOCK_RECHECK_DAYS` pass; a purchase reported mined while the owner is still the lock coin, or in the mempool while it has moved, is two hsd facts that disagree and no verdict. The database writes (`db/queries.rs::{mark_listing_sale_pending, sell_shakedex_listing}`) take the lock coin the purchase spends and move a listing only when that coin is the listing's stored lock outpoint: a listing without one is never moved by them (ReadyToFinalize, which never carries one, is not among their source states), and only `sell_listing_through_proven_lock` (R19) gives a listing an outpoint on sale. A Sold listing whose purchase hsd puts back in its mempool after a reorg (`mempool._removeBlock`: the lock coin 404, the owner the lock coin again, the purchase at -1) is SalePending again, unless another listing of the name is open by then; another purchase of the same lock coin mined instead replaces the sale's txid. Both are Sold's own guarded write (`resell_sold_listing`: from Sold only, for the listing's own lock outpoint), so Sold is in no other write's source set besides Unsell's (a reorg back, above), Resell's, and CancelMined's (a reorg replaced the winning purchase with a mined TRANSFER out of the stored lock coin to an address of ours, R28: the listing becomes CancelAwaitingFinalize and its sold txid is forgotten). A Listed, Restored or Cancelling listing whose lock coin is a coin and whose name has no live state (`info: null`), or whose name height is not its lock coin's (the name expired and was opened again), is Expired, keeping its lock outpoint (`expire_locked_listing`, from Listed, SalePending, Restored, Cancelling, CancelAwaitingFinalize or CancelFinalizing); with the lock coin spent, only `info: null` expires it, a SalePending listing as well (a purchase still in the mempool when the name expired), even when a real purchase spent it (the app closed until the buyer's registration lapsed): the listing is then Expired, not Sold, and the payment is in the wallet all the same. A Restored lock without a payment address (restored by name, R32) has no payment output to find a purchase by: it is Sold, with that txid, only on positive evidence in the transaction that spent its stored lock outpoint, the name's owner (read with `GET /tx`, or without the index in the block at the owner coin's height): mined; input k the stored lock outpoint, its witness `[signature, lock script]` with the signature's sighash type (its last byte) `0x84`, `ANYONECANPAY | SINGLEREVERSE`, which only a price step carries (our cancel is `0x83`); output k a TRANSFER of the name at the lock address; output `len - 1 - k`, the one `0x84` commits to, a payment (covenant NONE); and, as a cheap extra check, the TRANSFER's commitment not one of our derived addresses (`noncustodial/shakedex/sell.rs::sale_out_of_restored_lock`, `shakedex_jobs.rs::sale_of_restored_lock`; `sell::SpendView` carries each input's witness from both of hsd's shapes, REST `inputs[].witness` and `getblock` `vin[].txinwitness`). A commitment "not ours" alone is no evidence: after a restore the derived addresses are not complete (§5), so another device's cancel to a cancel address past this device's window would look like a sale. A price step commits to its payment output, so the sale was paid to that step's address, even though this device does not know it. Committing to an address of ours it is a cancel (R28) and is judged first; any other sighash, the mempool, another input coin, a transaction not found, or a lock input whose witness the reply leaves out or sends unreadable, is no verdict. If the buyer's FINALIZE is mined before a sync sees the purchase's TRANSFER as the owner, the owner's transaction is that FINALIZE, and following its input back to the TRANSFER is not done: the lock stays Restored (§5). **R25 — Keeping our listing on the market.** About hourly, `GET /listing/<name>/proof.json` is compared with the current step: re-upload unless it matches it semantically (same name, lock outpoint, public key and payment address; exactly one step with the same price, lock time and signature; no market fee address in effect, since `0x84` does not commit to a fee output; and the same `expiresAt`, since an earlier one hides the listing early). ## 4. Explicitly not enforced

- Nothing stops a seller and buyer agreeing a price elsewhere and using a plain Transfer and Finalize. That still works and needs none of this.
- `expiresAt` does not end a listing. Only a mined cancel does; the wallet says so wherever an expiry is shown.
- The market is not trusted for availability, fees or expiry, and is not needed for safety. Taking a listing off LearnHNS does not stop anyone holding the listing file from buying, at any step that is still valid — including a dearer one than the current step.
- A purchase is not protected against another buyer taking the same listing first. The loser pays nothing.
- No automatic purchase when a reverse auction reaches a price.
- ADR 0003's "no `hns-swap` dependency" is checked by a test that fails if `Cargo.lock` contains it.

## 5. Known gaps

- **Ledger, watch-only and extended-private-key profiles.** No buying or selling (R16, R29).
- **Background jobs need a running process.** Republishing, stepping reverse auctions and finalize reminders run while the app or `namehold-syncd` runs. The finalize reminders (purchase, listing, cancel) are sent by `namehold-syncd` while it runs, also with the app window closed, and by the app only while the daemon is not alive, so one process sends each; the reveal and renewal reminders run only in the app. The listing file keeps working regardless.
- **Recovery of a lock is automatic only once, and only with the address index.** R34 finds the locks of a restored profile once, from its address history on a node with `--index-tx` and `--index-address`, and adopts only names still in their lock. Without the index, a lock made later from another device of the same phrase, a lock still Locking or a cancel awaiting its finalize, the seller restores the name by hand (R32), presses "Find my locks" again, or imports the saved listing file. The search never uses an explorer: it needs the node anyway to adopt a lock, and it would hand the profile's addresses to a third party. Restore by name does not adopt a name still Locking or a cancel awaiting its finalize (R32), so after a restore a name still Locking is left to the ordinary Cancel transfer, and the wallet offers no FINALIZE for a cancel that was awaiting its finalize.
- **A restore sees addresses up to the gap limit past the last used one.** A restore provisions `address_gap_limit` addresses per branch (1 to `secure_wallet::MAX_GAP_LIMIT`, 1000: the setting is renderer-writable and every sync derives and looks up that many addresses, so a larger value is clamped and Settings refuses it), and every node sync grows each branch until that many unused addresses follow the highest used one (an address with coins or a transaction history on the node, or reserved here), scanning the new ones in the same sync. Reservations are marked used only on the device that made them: more than `address_gap_limit` reservations in a row with nothing on chain yet hide the addresses past them from another device until the window is raised. An address whose coins were all spent counts as used only through its transaction history, which hsd serves only with `--index-tx` as well as `--index-address`: with the address index alone only addresses that still hold coins move the window, so a run of more than `address_gap_limit` spent addresses ends it. On a node without `--index-address` the window does not grow. A sync whose node is not authoritative (SPV, or a full node that is behind or unreachable) reads balance and names from the explorer over the stored addresses, so it first grows each branch the same way from the explorer: an address the explorer lists a transaction for counts as used, spent or not, with no first-seen height (the explorer's tx list gives none), and the explorer is asked as the explorer discovery asks it, so five failures in a row end the growth for that sync; one sync grows a branch at most five rounds from the explorer (the explorer is not checked against the chain), Stop ends it before its next query, and a network without an explorer (only mainnet has one by default) leaves the window to the node.
- **A dead FINALIZE's sale is not found without the transaction index once the buyer has finalized.** A listing back at ReadyToFinalize (or still Locking) whose lock was finalized, bought and finalized by the buyer before any sync stays as it is, logged on every sync, on a node without `--index-tx`: our FINALIZE is then readable neither with `GET /tx` nor at `info.renewal`, which names the buyer's FINALIZE (R19).
- **A restored lock has no listing details.** Price steps, schedule and payment address are off-chain. A Restored lock (R32) shows "Locked — listing details unknown: import its listing file, or cancel"; the saved listing file is the backup. A lock restored by name has no payment address; it is followed to Sold through the name's owner only, on a price step's `0x84` signature over its lock coin (R22, R32). If the buyer's FINALIZE is mined before a sync sees the purchase's TRANSFER as the name's owner, the owner's transaction is that FINALIZE, and following its input back to the TRANSFER is not done: the lock stays Restored until its file is imported, or for good.
- **A cancel and the FINALIZE into its lock both undone by a reorg are not judged.** When a reorg takes the cancel's TRANSFER and the FINALIZE into the lock out of every block and the mempool, hsd answers 404 for both the cancel and the lock coin and the name's owner is the lock TRANSFER: the listing stays in its cancel state with no verdict until one of them is mined again. A Cancelled listing whose FINALIZE home a reorg took out together with both stays Cancelled likewise (the owner is neither its cancel's TRANSFER nor its lock coin), and one is re-checked only within `AFTER_LOCK_RECHECK_DAYS` of its last write, as a Sold one is.
- **A FINALIZE home sent from another device, after which the name moved on before a sync,** leaves this device's listing awaiting the cancel's finalize: the name's owner is then that later coin, and the FINALIZE is not followed back.
- **A cancel already finalized home before a sync needs the transaction index.** A listing whose cancel was mined and finalized home (from another device with the same seed) before this device saw the cancel is found from the FINALIZE back to its TRANSFER, which is read with `GET /tx` only (its block is not known): on a node without `--index-tx` that read finds nothing, so the listing stays as it was until a node with the index is used.
- **One device per listing.** Devices or profiles sharing a seed derive the same lock keys but not the same listing state; their background jobs would fight over uploads, and one device's Lower price is invisible to the other.
- **A node without a transaction index sees less.** The app's own hsd runs with `--index-tx`; a remote node may not. Without it hsd does not find a mined purchase, so the purchase is followed through its TRANSFER coin and the name's owner instead. A purchase lost while paying nothing and finalized since is shown as owned only once its own transaction is found, which needs the index: on such a node it stays lost, though the name arrives through normal sync. A sale is found without it, through the name's owner while the purchase's TRANSFER is unspent, and after the buyer's FINALIZE from the block our payment coin was seen mined in; a payment coin the wallet only ever saw in the mempool, spent before it was seen mined, leaves such a listing unjudged.
- **No fee bump.** hsd has no replace-by-fee; an underpaid purchase can only be helped by spending its change (CPFP), which the wallet does not offer.
- **No keep-alive for long listings.** The lock key could restart the renewal clock by transferring the lock coin to itself and finalizing, at the cost of re-signing every step; not offered.
- **Pending listings can be overwritten by anyone** on LearnHNS; only active listings are defended (R25).
- **An expired listing is not reported to LearnHNS Market.** `refresh-status` knows only a sale and a cancel, and the market drops a listing at its own `expiresAt` (`GET /listing/<name>/proof.json` answers 404 once it is past). Nothing is sent for an Expired listing.
- **A reorg after the market was told is not corrected there.** Once LearnHNS Market has recorded a sale or a cancel, a reorg that swaps the winning transaction (a purchase for our cancel, one cancel for another, or a mined sale back to the mempool) is not corrected there: its refresh-status only changes active or sale-pending listings. A reorg that returns the listing to Listed resets the market bookkeeping, and the listing is uploaded afresh.
- **Shared lock key on a 31-bit hash collision.** Harmless for funds; listing state is keyed by name and outpoint, never by address. It links the two names publicly.
- **One market.** Only LearnHNS Market. Other Shakedex channels, and a censorship-resistant fallback (Nostr relays), are roadmap items.
